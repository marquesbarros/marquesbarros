Profile

In my doctoral research, I study rock magnetism and paleointensity, focusing on the stability of magnetic records in geological materials and the characterisation of remanence carriers. I work at the Laboratório de Paleomagnetismo e Magnetismo de Rochas (USPMAG), Universidade de São Paulo.

My education in Geology, Big Data and Computing is complemented by professional experience in cloud data engineering. I develop analysis, visualisation and processing tools, with a particular interest in scientific software and educational tools for geoscience.

Software and projects

RIIMA · Registration of images from the same field, affinity classification and user-controlled group review. It does not identify minerals automatically.

Petrodle · A prototype intended to reinforce chemical and petrographic relationships between minerals through identification challenges, for anyone seeking to practise identifying minerals quickly.

Mineralchemy · A mineralogy learning reinforcement prototype for anyone seeking to practise identifying minerals quickly, using educational combinations to practise relationships in mineral composition, formation and transformation.
