01 / Doctoral research

A different view of the South Atlantic Anomaly.

As volcanic rocks crystallise, they preserve records of the strength of the Earth's magnetic field.

In my Geophysics PhD at Universidade de São Paulo, I investigate how to improve the acquisition of these records. The South Atlantic Anomaly is a region of low present-day field intensity. Reconstructing changes over time helps us investigate the recent evolution of the geomagnetic field. The project aims to produce paleointensity data to contribute to this reconstruction.

02 / Geology dissertation

Machine learning applied to geological mapping.

In my undergraduate Geology dissertation, I used machine learning to investigate mafic-ultramafic bodies in the southern Brasília Belt.

The study combined a geological map, Landsat 9 imagery, SRTM elevation data and airborne geophysical products for lithological classification. The model was used to test whether patterns associated with mapped geological units could be recognised.

The probability map was compared with field observations. Predictions support geological interpretation and need to be checked against local evidence.

Laboratory work

Digital petrography in the laboratory.

As a fellowship holder, I work with the laboratory’s petrography instruments. My activities include configuring acquisitions and assessing the quality of images obtained with the AxioScan 7.

Curiosity and the need to consolidate relationships between minerals, their composition and their petrographic contexts motivated me to develop the prototypes presented below.

Scientific software · Python

RIIMA

Really Intuitive Image Mapping by Affinity

Developed to support interpretation of petrographic images of the same field of view. RIIMA registers images, classifies pixels by optical affinity, and lets users organise and review groups under their control.

The initial analysis uses K-means. During review, Keep/Remove examples guide refinement; the user checks and approves masks. Groups do not automatically correspond to mineral species.

Python · OpenCV · affinity classification · digital petrography

In development, with a desktop interface, a Python core, and documentation covering the method, use and limitations.

Mineralogy · Learning reinforcement

Petrodle

A mineralogy learning reinforcement prototype intended for anyone seeking to practise identifying minerals quickly. Identification challenges bring together chemical properties, photomicrographs and optical descriptions to practise relationships between minerals and their petrographic contexts.

Mineralchemy

A mineralogy learning reinforcement prototype intended for anyone seeking to practise identifying minerals quickly. Combining components and consulting mineral entries is designed to help consolidate relationships between chemical composition, mineral formation and transformation.
